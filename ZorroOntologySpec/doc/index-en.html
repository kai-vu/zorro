Ontology Specification Draft

Abstract

We developed an upper-level ontology for fault diagnosis in cyber-physical systems that captures the essential concepts and relations required for efficient diagnostic. The ontology was created through analysis of public and proprietary fault datasets, consultations with domain experts from two industrial partners, and a review of existing reference ontologies such as IDO and IOF-MRO. By modeling both operational and functional dependencies, often overlooked in prior work, Zorro enables system-level causal reasoning and better representation of failure propagation.

The namespace for Zorro terms is http://w3id.org/ZorroOntology/.

The Zorro ontology is available at http://w3id.org/ZorroOntology/ .

Zorro Ontology: Description back to ToC

Zorro is an ontology designed as the foundational framework for fault diagnosis in cyber-physical systems. Its development involved extensive analysis of diverse input sources to distill the most valuable knowledge, with carefully curated concepts and terminology to ensure clarity and consistency throughout. The ontology draws inspiration from IDO and IOF‑MRO: IDO—approved by the ISO TC 184/SC4 Industrial Data committee in July 2023—is notable in industrial standardization, while IOF‑MRO supports semantic interoperability in the maintenance domain through modular ontologies. The figure above illustrates the Zorro ontology’s design. A Component can represent a part, assembly, or subsystem, each performing a specific Function. When a component malfunctions, a Problem arises. Each problem has one or more causes and leads to a specific Effect. To address these issues, two types of Procedure are available: a Solution, which directly resolves the problem, and a Workaround, which mitigates the resulting effects. Both procedure types comprise multiple Steps, with each step involving a specific part of the system
